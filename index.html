About CropScope

How this diagnostic console works, end to end.

Pipeline
  1. CaptureA leaf photo is uploaded and resized to 160×160 RGB.
  2. BackboneEfficientNet-B0, pretrained on ImageNet, extracts visual features.
  3. HeadGlobal average pooling → dropout → a 39-way softmax classifier.
  4. ReadoutThe top prediction and two runner-ups are surfaced with confidence scores.
Coverage

Trained on a PlantVillage-style leaf dataset spanning 14 crops — apple, blueberry, cherry, corn, grape, orange, peach, bell pepper, potato, raspberry, soybean, squash, strawberry, and tomato — across 39 total classes including healthy baselines.

Reading the gauge

The dial color reflects diagnosis, not just confidence: teal for a healthy call, amber for a lower-confidence or moderate call, coral for a disease diagnosis. This is a student project — not a substitute for agronomic advice.
